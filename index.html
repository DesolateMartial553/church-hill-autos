Your MOT, explained

A little preparation before your visit to Station Rd can make the next steps clearer.

What's checked in an MOT 8 systems

  • Lights
  • Brakes
  • Tyres
  • Steering
  • Suspension
  • Seatbelts
  • Emissions
  • Windscreen and wipers

These are some of the checks on a car MOT. The test checks safety and environmental standards; it does not replace a service.

Read the full DVSA checklist.

If your car fails Retest rules

Use the failure report to discuss the repairs needed and arrange a retest.

Under standard DVSA rules, leaving your car at the test centre for repair means a free partial retest if it is retested within 10 working days.

If you take it away for repairs, returning to the same centre within 10 working days can mean a partial retest fee. Certain items qualify for a free retest by the end of the next working day. Otherwise, a full test and fee apply.

We explain the failure report, quote for the repairs and arrange your retest. Leave your car with us for repair and we’ll complete the partial retest free within 10 working days.

Check the DVSA retest rules.

Before your MOT

  • Check that your lights work and your windscreen is clean.
  • Top up the screenwash and check that the wipers clear the glass.
  • Look for visible tyre damage and make sure seatbelts are accessible.

Have your registration and mileage ready when you book your visit to Station Rd.

MOT due date reminder

Not sure when yours runs out? Check your MOT history and expiry date on GOV.UK, then set a reminder in your calendar to call Church Hill Autos before it is due.
